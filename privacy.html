PRIVACY POLICY

This is the privacy policy of Chumbley’s Auto Detailing for chumbleysdetailing.com. It covers the website, nothing else.

ASKING FOR A QUOTE

The quote form doesn’t send anything to us on its own. Press SEND IT! and it opens your own email app with a message to chumbleysdetailing@gmail.com already filled in: your name, phone, email, vehicle, the date you’d like, the service and your note. Nothing leaves your device until you send that email yourself.

When you press the button, the page also tells Google Analytics that a quote was started, with the service you picked and nothing else from the form.

WHAT OUR LOG KEEPS

Our site writes down each page view on Cloudflare before any script runs in your browser:

  • the page, and the site, search or ad that sent you, if there was one;
  • your kind of device and your browser;
  • the town, state, country and internet provider of your connection, as Cloudflare estimates them.

It never keeps your IP address or anything that names you. It also counts taps on our phone number and email address, and how many seconds a page stays open.

COOKIES

The site sets one cookie of its own, ts_src, when your visit starts. It remembers where the visit came from (the referring site or ad, the first page, your kind of device and the time), holds no identifier, and is gone when you close your browser. Google Analytics sets _ga and _ga_EQVL6SGTES.

GOOGLE ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics as a second count of visits. It starts once the page has loaded, or at your first tap, and sends Google the pages you view and how you found us, under Google’s privacy policy. Google’s opt-out add-on, or blocking cookies for this site, switches it off.

OTHER SERVICES

Cloudflare hosts the site and, like any host, sees your IP address when it sends you a page. The fonts, the pictures and the intro video come from this site itself. Our Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google Maps links take you to those services, where their own policies apply.

QUESTIONS?

Call or text (615) 670-3379, or email chumbleysdetailing@gmail.com, to ask what we have from you or to have it deleted.
