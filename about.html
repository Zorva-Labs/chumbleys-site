THE ORIGIN STORY

MEET MADDOX!

BORN WITH THE GIFT OF SHINE

MADDOX didn't get bitten by a radioactive microfiber towel. He didn't fall into a vat of paint sealant. He just cared — cared about every door jamb, every wheel well, every cup-holder crumb, and every inch of leather other shops skipped over.

Born and raised in Gallatin, TN, Maddox grew up tinkering on family cars long before he could legally drive one. What started as Saturday wash sessions in the driveway turned into a full-on obsession with making vehicles look better than the day they rolled off the lot.

At Chumbley's, "good enough" doesn't cut it. Every car gets the same hours-long, end-to-end attention — whether it's the daily driver hauling soccer practice or the weekend showpiece that lives under a cover. Paint, plastics, leather, glass, wheels, vents, every nook the previous detailer skipped — it all gets the treatment.

Honest pricing. Mobile service. No upsells. No surprises. Just a young hometown hero who treats your car like it's his own.

YOUR MISSION, SHOULD YOU CHOOSE TO ACCEPT: Hand him your keys and let him work his magic.
