3 · Google Analytics

Google’s own tag, measured in the visitor’s browser.

Why this differs from section 1
It will read lower than section 1, and that is not an error. A browser-side tag never sees a visit where:
  • an ad or tracking blocker stopped it loading — routinely a fifth to a third of visitors;
  • a privacy browser or network-level blocker stripped it before it ran;
  • the person left before it finished loading;
  • Google judged the visit automated and filtered it out, which occasionally catches real people.
Our own log in section 1 is written before any script runs at all, which is why both are here.
